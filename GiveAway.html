Monarchs at Play – Steam Next Fest Challenge Giveaway

1. Organizer

The organizer of the Monarchs at Play Steam Next Fest Challenge Giveaway is:

MixedMoonGames GmbH
Germany

Further company and contact information can be found in our Imprint.

This giveaway is organized solely by MixedMoonGames GmbH. It is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Valve Corporation, Steam, or Discord.

2. Giveaway Period

The giveaway takes place during the Steam Next Fest from October 19, 2026 until October 26, 2026.

Only Challenge runs completed and entries submitted during this period are eligible.

Participation is free of charge. No purchase is necessary.

3. How to Participate

To participate in the giveaway, players must:

  1. Play the free demo of Monarchs at Play on Steam.
  2. Play the in-game "Steam Next Fest Challenge".
  3. Successfully complete the Challenge by reaching the required score threshold.
  4. After successfully completing the Challenge, the game will generate a unique Challenge Code containing verification data for the achieved score.
  5. Join the official Monarchs at Play Discord server.
  6. Go to the "Next Fest Challenge" channel and submit a Challenge ticket containing the unique Challenge Code generated by the game.

A valid Challenge ticket must be submitted before the end of the giveaway period. The Discord account used to submit the ticket will be considered the participant's account for the purposes of the giveaway.

Submitting a Challenge Code does not guarantee a prize.

Players may play the Challenge multiple times and improve their score. If a participant submits multiple valid scores, only their highest valid score will be considered for the leaderboard.

Each individual may participate in the random prize draw only once, regardless of the number of Challenge runs, codes, tickets, or Discord accounts used.

4. Prizes

A total of 15 Steam keys for the full version of Monarchs at Play will be awarded.

Top 5 Players

The five participants with the highest verified scores at the end of the giveaway period will each receive one Steam key for the full version of Monarchs at Play.

If two or more participants achieve the same score, the participant who submitted the respective score first will rank higher.

10 Random Winners

After the Top 5 have been determined, ten additional winners will be selected at random from all remaining eligible participants who submitted a valid Challenge ticket.

Players who have already won a key as one of the Top 5 will not participate in the random draw.

Each participant can win a maximum of one Steam key.

5. Verification and Fair Play

All submitted Challenge Codes may be verified by MixedMoonGames GmbH before a participant or score is considered eligible.

Manipulation of Challenge Codes, game data or scores, the use of cheats or exploits, or any other attempt to gain an unfair advantage is prohibited.

MixedMoonGames GmbH reserves the right to exclude entries where there is reasonable evidence of manipulation, cheating, fraudulent participation, the use of multiple accounts to obtain additional entries, or another violation of these Terms & Conditions.

Invalid or unverifiable Challenge Codes will not be considered.

6. Winner Selection and Notification

After the giveaway period has ended, MixedMoonGames GmbH will verify the submitted entries and determine the five highest valid scores.

The ten additional winners will then be selected randomly from the remaining valid participants.

Winners will be contacted directly via Discord using the Discord account associated with their Challenge ticket.

Winners must respond to the winner notification within 7 days. If a winner cannot be contacted or does not respond within this period, MixedMoonGames GmbH reserves the right to select another eligible participant as a replacement winner.

7. Prize Distribution

Each winner will receive one Steam key for the full version of Monarchs at Play.

The Steam key can be activated on Steam and grants access to the full version of Monarchs at Play upon its release.

The prize cannot be exchanged for cash or another prize and may not be sold by the winner.

8. Eligibility

Participation is open to individuals who are permitted to participate under the laws applicable to them.

Participants must also meet the minimum age requirements applicable to the use of Steam and Discord in their country. Where required by applicable law, minors may only participate with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

Employees of MixedMoonGames GmbH and persons directly involved in the development of Monarchs at Play or the organization of this giveaway are excluded from participation.

MixedMoonGames GmbH reserves the right to exclude participation where required by applicable law.

9. Data Protection

For the purpose of conducting the giveaway, MixedMoonGames GmbH processes the information submitted through the Challenge ticket, in particular the participant's Discord account and Challenge Code, as well as the score information associated with that code.

This information is used exclusively to verify participation, determine the winners, contact them and distribute the prizes.

The data collected specifically for the giveaway will be deleted after the giveaway has been completed and the prizes have been distributed, unless there is a legal obligation to retain certain information.

Further information about the processing of personal data and the rights of participants can be found in our Privacy Policy.

10. Discord

A Discord account and membership in the official Monarchs at Play Discord server are required to submit a Challenge entry and to receive winner notifications.

Discord is used solely as the platform for submitting entries and communicating with participants. Discord Inc. is not the organizer, sponsor or administrator of this giveaway and is not responsible for its execution or the distribution of prizes.

Participants must comply with Discord's applicable Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

MixedMoonGames GmbH reserves the right to modify, suspend or cancel the giveaway if circumstances beyond its reasonable control prevent the giveaway from being conducted as intended, including technical failures, security issues or attempts to manipulate the Challenge or its results.

Any changes will not unreasonably disadvantage participants who have already entered the giveaway.

German law applies to the extent permitted by applicable law. Mandatory consumer protection laws applicable in a participant's country of residence remain unaffected.

If individual provisions of these Terms & Conditions are or become invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected.
